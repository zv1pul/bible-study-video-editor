Drop your series artwork in here and the app will use it automatically —
no need to upload it again each session.

  intro.png   shown for 5 seconds at the very start   (e.g. the "Exile & Return
              — A Time to Build" title slide)
  outro.png   shown for 5 seconds at the very end
  logo.png    placed in the reserved 100x100 square on every point card

.jpg and .webp work too. Anything uploaded in the sidebar overrides these.

Make the intro and outro the same shape as your video (16:9 for a normal
widescreen recording) so they fill the frame instead of being letterboxed.
