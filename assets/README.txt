Drop your series artwork in here and the app uses it automatically — no need
to upload it again each session, and it travels with the app when deployed.

  intro.png   shown for 5 seconds at the very start   (e.g. the "Exile & Return
              — A Time to Build" title slide)
  outro.png   shown for 5 seconds at the very end
  logo.png    placed in the reserved 120x120 square on every point card

.jpg and .webp work too. Anything uploaded in the sidebar overrides these.

Make the intro and outro the same shape as your video (16:9 for a normal
widescreen recording) so they fill the frame instead of being letterboxed.

IMPORTANT for the hosted app: these files have to be committed to the
repository. A hosted deployment has no permanent storage of its own, so an
image that only exists on your laptop will not appear in videos rendered
there. Add the file here, commit, and push.
