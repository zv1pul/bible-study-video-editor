# Bible Study Video Editor

Upload a lesson recording, paste the outline, get back a video with the
points captioned at the right moments.

Free hosting notes: this Space runs on 2 vCPU / 16 GB RAM. Use the hosted
(Groq) transcription engine for anything longer than about 15 minutes.
