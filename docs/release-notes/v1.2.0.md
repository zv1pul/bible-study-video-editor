**Bible Study Video Editor 1.2.0** — turn a lesson recording and its outline into a finished teaching video: the speaker's name, a card for every point, discussion countdowns, and your opening and closing slides.

### Download the one for your computer
- **Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1, M2, M3, M4…) — *Mac (Apple Silicon)*
- **Older Intel Mac** — *Mac (Intel)*
- **Windows 10 or 11** — *Windows*

Not sure which Mac? Apple menu → About This Mac → "Chip". Unzip it and follow **Read me first** inside.

### Nothing to set up
No account and no key — the AI that finds each point is already connected. Recordings are listened to on your own computer and are never uploaded.

### What's new
- **Your settings stay put** — discussion time, video quality, and your opening and closing slides (replace one and it's used for every video until you change it; PDFs work too)
- **Past speakers are one click away**
- **Choose the video…** opens Finder or File Explorer
- **Group discussions are cut cleanly** — when the teacher sends the room into groups, the video goes from the question, to the countdown, straight to the teacher calling everyone back
- **Every video gets its own file** in Movies / Videos → *Bible Study Video Editor* — nothing is overwritten
- **Start the next video** clears the lesson and keeps your settings
- Closing the browser tab stops the app a few minutes later (never in the middle of a render)

### Fixed
Long countdowns failing on Windows, a render that could freeze on a glitchy recording, downloads that restarted after a dropped connection, the AI step waiting with no limit, and more — every one now has an automatic check.

Updates install themselves, each checked on your computer before it's used.
