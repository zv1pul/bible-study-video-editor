**Bible Study Video Editor 1.2.1** — turn a lesson recording and its outline into a finished teaching video: the speaker's name, a card for every point, discussion countdowns, and your opening and closing slides.

### Download the one for your computer
- **Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1, M2, M3, M4…) — *Mac Apple Silicon*
- **Older Intel Mac** — *Mac Intel*
- **Windows 10 or 11** — *Windows*

Not sure which Mac? Apple menu → About This Mac → "Chip". Unzip it and follow **Read me first** inside.

### Nothing to set up
No account and no key — the AI that finds each point is already connected, and the usual settings (30-second discussions, the Romans slides, trims, full-screen cards) are already in place. Recordings are listened to on your own computer and are never uploaded.

### New in 1.2.1
- **Paste the whole outline at once** — paste it as it was emailed; the takeaway, divisions, principles and questions are sorted into their boxes for you to check
- **Preset speakers** — the group's teachers come ready to pick; save a new speaker with one click, change a name or title after picking and keep the change with *Update preset*
- **Lesson title** — names the finished video file ("Romans 3 21-26 - Lesson 5 - captioned.mp4")
- **Slides one at a time** — replace, remove, or put back the opening slide, the closing slide and the logo separately; a replaced slide stays your default, through updates
- **Every setting stays as you left it**, including which AI service and engines are used
- If a key of your own stops working, the app says so and offers to go back to the shared keys

### Checked on every kind of computer
Every release is opened and used on a real Apple Silicon Mac, Intel Mac and Windows PC before it is published — a whole lesson analysed and rendered, plus the mistakes people make (a wrong file, a bad link, damaged settings), none of which may crash the app.

Updates install themselves, each checked on your computer before it's used.
