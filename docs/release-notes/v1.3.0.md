**Bible Study Video Editor 1.3.0** — turn a lesson recording and its outline into a finished teaching video: the speaker's name, a card for every point, discussion countdowns, and your opening and closing slides.

### Download the one for your computer
- **Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1, M2, M3, M4…) — *Mac Apple Silicon*
- **Older Intel Mac** — *Mac Intel*
- **Windows 10 or 11** — *Windows*

Not sure which Mac? Apple menu → About This Mac → "Chip". Unzip it and follow **Read me first** inside.

### Nothing to set up
No account and no key, and the usual settings are already in place. Recordings are listened to on your own computer and are never uploaded.

### New in 1.3.0
- **The video checks itself** — after every render the app watches and listens to the finished video: the length, the slides, every card and countdown, what's heard just before and after each countdown, the start and the end. Anything that looks wrong is explained, and the common problems come with a one-click fix.
- **Ask the helper** — ask about the app or the lesson ("When does he say the takeaway?"), or ask for a change in plain words ("make question 2's countdown 45 seconds"). Every change is shown first and only happens when you press Apply; Undo puts it back.
- **Report a problem** — makes a small file (never the video or any key) to send if something goes wrong.

### Safety rails
The helper can only make a short list of known changes, kept inside the recording; it never touches a file, has its own small daily allowance so it can't use up what analysing lessons needs, and treats what's said in the recording as material, never as instructions. A fix never changes a video — it changes the plan, and the next render is saved as a new file.

Updates install themselves, each checked on your computer before it's used.
