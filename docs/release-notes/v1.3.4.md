**Bible Study Video Editor 1.3.4** — turn a lesson recording and its outline into a finished teaching video: the speaker's name, a card for every point, discussion countdowns, and your opening and closing slides.

### Download the one for your computer
- **Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1, M2, M3, M4…) — *Mac Apple Silicon*
- **Older Intel Mac** — *Mac Intel*
- **Windows 10 or 11** — *Windows*

Not sure which Mac? Apple menu → About This Mac → "Chip". Unzip it and follow **Read me first** inside.

### Nothing to set up
No account and no key, and the usual settings are already in place. Recordings are listened to on your own computer and are never uploaded.

### New in 1.3.4 — scripture written any way
- **Put the reference wherever you like** — above the verses, below them, or at the end in brackets or after a dash. With or without blank lines.
- **All the usual forms work** — Romans 1:5, 1:5-6, 1:5b, 1:5b-6, 1:5,8, Romans 5:12, 21b, Rom. 5:12; 6:23, 1 Cor 13:4-7 — even "Romans 5,12" with a comma.
- **Copied straight from a Bible app** — verse numbers are tidied away, and a long passage can be several paragraphs.
- **It shows what it found** — under the scripture box, with a warning if a passage has no reference. A "Scripture" section in a pasted outline fills the box by itself.

### New in 1.3.3 — from the group's feedback
- **Timings in minutes and seconds** — the table shows 2:51.3 instead of 171.3; type either.
- **Questions aren't cut short** — the countdown now waits for the teacher's first pause after a question, so "…three minutes" is heard before the timer, not after it.
- **"BSF" in titles** — e.g. NY Men's BSF Substitute Teaching Leader.
- **Half verses work** — scripture like Romans 4:16a or 4:16b–17a is recognised.
- **Cards wrap tidily** — a reference like (Romans 4:18-25) is never split across lines, and short text stays on one line. You can also change a card's words in the timings table (type | to start a new line) without losing its countdown.

### New in 1.3.2
- **Leave it running in the background** — with the computer asleep or the tab in the background, even mid-video, the page now reconnects by itself with all your work (before, the app could shut itself down and the page wouldn't load).
- **Pick up where you left off** — if a page ever opens blank, one button brings back the recording, outline, timings and finished video.

### New in 1.3.1
- **No more "This site can't be reached"** — the app now always uses the same address, so a browser tab left open reconnects by itself after a restart, an update or the computer sleeping.

### New in 1.3.0
- **The video checks itself** — after every render the app watches and listens to the finished video: the length, the slides, every card and countdown, what's heard just before and after each countdown, the start and the end. Anything that looks wrong is explained, and the common problems come with a one-click fix.
- **Ask the helper** — ask about the app or the lesson ("When does he say the takeaway?"), or ask for a change in plain words ("make question 2's countdown 45 seconds"). Every change is shown first and only happens when you press Apply; Undo puts it back.
- **Report a problem** — makes a small file (never the video or any key) to send if something goes wrong.

### Safety rails
The helper can only make a short list of known changes, kept inside the recording; it never touches a file, has its own small daily allowance so it can't use up what analysing lessons needs, and treats what's said in the recording as material, never as instructions. A fix never changes a video — it changes the plan, and the next render is saved as a new file.

Updates install themselves, each checked on your computer before it's used.
