Bible Study Video Editor — Windows
==================================

1.  Unzip the download: right-click it -> "Extract All…" -> Extract.
    Put the folder anywhere you like (Documents is fine).

2.  Open the folder and double-click  "Start Bible Study Video Editor".

    THE FIRST TIME ONLY, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC".
    That is expected for any app not bought from the Microsoft Store.
    Click "More info", then "Run anyway". It won't ask again.

3.  The app opens in your web browser. That is normal — it runs entirely
    on your own computer and your recordings are never uploaded anywhere.

    The first time, it checks that everything works on this computer
    (about half a minute). There is nothing to set up — no account and no
    key; the AI that finds each point is already connected.

Using it:   choose the recording, pick the speaker (people who have spoken
            before are remembered), paste the outline, press Analyse,
            glance over the timings, press Render.

Finished videos are saved to:   Videos > Bible Study Video Editor
            Each video gets its own file — nothing is ever overwritten.

Your settings stay as you left them: discussion time, video quality, and
your opening and closing slides (replace one in the left-hand panel and it
is used for every video until you change it again).

Updates install themselves: each one is checked on your computer before
it is used, and if anything goes wrong the app goes back to the version
that worked.

You can leave it in a background tab, or let the computer sleep, even in
the middle of a video: the page reconnects by itself with all your work.
If a page ever opens blank, press "Pick up where I left off".

To close the app: press "Quit the app" at the bottom of the left-hand
panel — or just close its tab; it stops by itself some hours later (never
during a job). Opening it again while it is running brings the page back.

If anything misbehaves, press "Check this computer" in the left-hand panel —
it says plainly what's wrong.
