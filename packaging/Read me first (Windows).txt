Bible Study Video Editor — Windows
==================================

1.  Unzip the download: right-click it -> "Extract All…" -> Extract.
    Put the folder anywhere you like (Documents is fine).

2.  Open the folder and double-click  "Start Bible Study Video Editor".

    THE FIRST TIME ONLY, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC".
    That is expected for any app not bought from the Microsoft Store.
    Click "More info", then "Run anyway". It won't ask again.

3.  The app opens in your web browser. That is normal — it runs entirely
    on your own computer and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    The first time, it checks that everything works on this computer
    (about half a minute), then asks for a free Google AI key — the box
    explains how to get one in a minute, no card needed.

Finished videos are saved to:   Videos > Bible Study Video Editor

Updates install themselves: each one is checked on your computer before
it is used, and if anything goes wrong the app goes back to the version
that worked.

To close the app: "Quit the app" at the bottom of the left-hand panel.

If anything misbehaves, press "Check this computer" in the left-hand panel —
it says plainly what's wrong.
