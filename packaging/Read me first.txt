Bible Study Video Editor — how to install
=========================================

1.  Drag "Bible Study Video Editor" into your Applications folder.

2.  THE FIRST TIME ONLY, open it like this:

        RIGHT-CLICK the app  ->  choose "Open"  ->  click "Open" again

    Do not double-click it the first time. macOS blocks apps that were not
    bought from the App Store until you open one this way once. You will see
    a warning saying the developer cannot be verified — that is expected,
    and clicking "Open" is what tells macOS you trust it.

    After that first time, double-clicking works normally, forever.

3.  The app opens in your web browser. That is normal — it runs entirely on
    your own computer and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    The very first time you analyse a lesson it downloads a speech model,
    which takes a minute. After that it is quick.

Finished videos are saved to:
    Movies > Bible Study Video Editor

Nothing else needs installing. Python, FFmpeg and the fonts are all inside
the app.

If you see "the app is damaged and can't be opened", it was unzipped by
something that stripped its permissions. Download it again and unzip it by
double-clicking the .zip in Finder.
