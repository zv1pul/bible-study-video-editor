Bible Study Video Editor — Mac
==============================

1.  Double-click the .zip if it hasn't unzipped already, then drag
    "Bible Study Video Editor" into your Applications folder.

2.  THE FIRST TIME ONLY, macOS will refuse to open it. That is expected —
    it blocks any app that wasn't bought from the App Store. Do this once:

        a) Double-click the app. A message says it "was not opened" or
           "cannot be verified". Click "Done".
        b) Open System Settings  ->  Privacy & Security  ->  scroll down
           to the Security section. You'll see the app's name with an
           "Open Anyway" button. Click it (within an hour of step a, or
           repeat step a).
        c) Enter your Mac password when asked, then click "Open".

    After that, double-clicking works normally, for good.
    (On macOS 14 or earlier: right-click the app and choose "Open" instead.)

3.  The app opens in your web browser. That's normal — it runs entirely on
    your own computer and your recordings are never uploaded anywhere.

    The first time, it checks that everything works on this computer (about
    half a minute), then asks for a free Google AI key — the box explains
    how to get one in a minute, no card needed.

Finished videos are saved to:   Movies > Bible Study Video Editor

Updates install themselves: each one is checked on your computer before it
is used, and if anything goes wrong the app goes back to the version that
worked.

To close the app: "Quit the app" at the bottom of the left-hand panel.

If anything misbehaves, press "Check this computer" in the left-hand panel —
it says plainly what's wrong.
